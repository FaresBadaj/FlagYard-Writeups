## How to get the flag

The flag is shown as an **on-screen subtitle** inside one of the videos. You just
play it and read the screen.

1. Open the videos folder in **VLC**.
2. Play **`Matryoshka (9).mkv`** (video number **9**).
3. Skip to **second 24** (`00:24`).
4. The **subtitle text on screen** is the flag.

**Flag:** `FlagY{my_font_amazing_isnt_it}`

> On screen it renders as `flagy{my_font_amazing_isnt_it}` — read it as
> `FlagY{my_font_amazing_isnt_it}`.
